\grantentry
  {Enabling Flexible Hydropower Operation by AI-Based Active Flow Control}
  {ÅForsk Foundation}{2027 -- 2031 (5 years)}{4\,000\,000 SEK}{\textbf{PI}}
  {Awarded by the ÅForsk Foundation through their Early-Career Research Grant
   program. The project combines CFD, reinforcement learning-based active flow
   control, and hydropower to enable more flexible hydropower operation. A PhD
   student will be recruited at Linköping University. The total project budget
   including departmental co-funding is 5\,550\,000 SEK.}
