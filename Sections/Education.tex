\cventry{PhD}{University of Tehran, Mechanical Engineering, Hydraulic Machinery}{2012 -- 2018}
\begin{cvbody}
\textit{Thesis:} Uncertainty quantification of turbulent flows in hydraulic machinery\\
\textit{Supervisors:} Prof.\ Mehrdad Raisee, Prof.\ Michel Cervantes, Prof.\ Ahmad Nourbakhsh\\
Extended uncertainty quantification (UQ) to hydraulic machinery using polynomial chaos expansion, and developed efficient techniques, namely compressed sensing and multifidelity $\ell_1$ minimization, to overcome the high computational cost of traditional UQ, validated on both analytical and CFD problems. Case studies showed that combined operational and geometrical uncertainties significantly influence the flow and performance of hydraulic pumps, contributing to more robust and reliable machinery design.
\end{cvbody}
